# 1\. Platform Identity and Purpose

Capimax PropShare is Capimax’s digital, non-blockchain platform for structured fractional participation in real-estate projects, with a primary focus on off-plan and under-construction properties and portfolios. It converts an approved property participation into digitally administered units recorded in a central ownership ledger. It combines onboarding, due diligence, investment documentation, payments, installment administration, construction updates, ownership records, reporting and controlled exit services in one operating environment.

**CORE POSITIONING  
**PropShare units are digitally recorded contractual participation interests. They are not cryptocurrencies and are not blockchain tokens. The authoritative position is established by the applicable SPV or issuing structure, signed agreements and the approved holder register.

## What the platform delivers

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **Capability** | **Platform output** |
| Property access | Curated participation opportunities with structured information, financial analysis, legal files, valuation and risk disclosures. |
| Fractional participation | Defined units, minimum participation amounts and digitally maintained allocations. |
| Transaction execution | Order, payment, contract, allocation and certificate workflows with reconciliation. |
| Lifecycle administration | Installments, project milestones, notices, statements, distributions and document versioning. |
| Controlled liquidity | Internal secondary-market listing and liquidity-provider routes, subject to eligibility and available demand. |
| Multi-party operations | Dedicated workflows for investors, developers, brokers, liquidity providers, institutions, service partners and administrators. |

## Platform mission

To make professionally governed real-estate participation easier to access, administer and monitor while preserving clear records, defined responsibilities and asset-level documentation.

# 10\. Legal Ownership, SPV, Ledger and Certificate

Each opportunity uses an asset-specific legal and recordkeeping structure. The applicable SPV or issuing entity, participation agreements and authoritative holder register define the investor’s legal and economic rights. The platform ledger administers unit availability and reconciles those rights with transactions and supporting evidence.

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **Layer** | **Authoritative function** |
| Property/title record | Shows the legally recognized ownership, title or contractual rights relating to the underlying property. |
| SPV / issuing structure | Holds or administers the asset or relevant contractual interest and defines governance, economics and obligations. |
| Participation agreement | Defines the investor’s rights, payment duties, fees, restrictions, distributions, transfers and exit mechanics. |
| Holder register | Records the approved holders and their recognized interests. |
| PropShare unit ledger | Tracks issued, available, reserved, owned, transferred, cancelled and exited units. |
| Digital certificate | Provides a readable participation record linked to the holder, property, units, agreement and verification data. |
| Transaction record | Connects orders, payments, refunds, allocations, transfers, fees and reconciliation events. |

## Ledger controls

- No unit is allocated twice.
- Reservations expire or settle according to the order rules.
- Allocation follows cleared payment, accepted documents and compliance status.
- Approved transfers update both the platform ledger and the authoritative holder register.
- Ledger totals reconcile with order, payment, contract and certificate records.
- Corrections preserve the original event and add an authorized adjustment trail.

# 11\. Investment Process and Settlement

**1\.** Investor signs in, completes verification and opens an eligible property page.

**2\.** Investor reviews the property dossier, current documents, risks, fees and exit terms.

**3\.** Investor selects the unit quantity or participation amount and the available payment structure.

**4\.** The platform calculates investment principal, entry fee, installment schedule and total amount due.

**5\.** Investor accepts the participation agreement, disclosures and electronic signature records.

**6\.** The order receives a reservation reference and payment instructions.

**7\.** Payment is authorized and matched to the order.

**8\.** Compliance, payment and document checks complete.

**9\.** Units are allocated in the central ledger and the holder register is updated.

**10\.** The digital certificate, receipt, contract set and portfolio position appear in the investor dashboard.

**11\.** Ongoing statements, milestones, distributions and exit options remain linked to the holding.

## Order states

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **State** | **Meaning** |
| Started | Selection has begun but no reservation exists. |
| Reserved | Units are temporarily held against the order. |
| Payment processing | Payment authorization or bank matching is in progress. |
| Documentation complete | Required agreements and acknowledgments are recorded. |
| Allocated | Payment and controls are complete; units are registered. |
| Partially paid | An installment position is active under its schedule. |
| Cancelled / expired | The order ended under the applicable rules. |
| Refunded | An approved payment reversal is completed and reconciled. |

- The payer and beneficiary must match verified account and contractual records.
- Every payment carries an order, installment, fee, refund or withdrawal reference.
- Card, wallet, bank and digital-asset statuses are normalized into one reconciliation process.
- A crypto payment is a payment method; the resulting PropShare position remains a non-blockchain contractual participation recorded in the central ledger.
- Refunds follow the agreement, payment-channel rules, compliance controls and recorded approval workflow.
- Withdrawals are released only to verified payment details associated with the entitled account.

**LIQUIDITY DISCLOSURE  
**The platform provides controlled exit mechanisms; it does not guarantee a buyer, a quote, a sale date, a price, capital preservation or continuous liquidity. Execution depends on eligibility, documented rights, available demand and the selected route.

## Secondary-market sequence

**1\.** Seller selects an eligible holding and quantity.

**2\.** Platform checks holding status, restrictions, unpaid obligations and transfer eligibility.

**3\.** Seller enters price and validity period or accepts the applicable mechanism.

**4\.** Listing is published to eligible buyers.

**5\.** Buyer accepts price, documents, fees and risk disclosures.

**6\.** Buyer payment and compliance checks complete.

**7\.** Units and holder-register records transfer; fees and settlement records post to both accounts.

# 23\. Brokers, Referrals and Commissions

The broker module connects approved introducers to eligible platform opportunities without transferring investment-decision responsibility from the investor.

- Each broker receives a unique approved referral identifier or link.
- Attribution records connect the lead, consent, campaign, account and qualifying transaction.
- Commission becomes eligible only under the broker agreement and completed qualifying-event rules.

- Brokers use only approved, current property materials and never promise returns, liquidity, approval or allocation.
- The investor completes KYC, reviews the property dossier and makes the participation decision directly.

| --- | --- |
| **Stage** | **LP workflow** |
| Mandate | LP completes KYB, ownership, authority and approved market mandate. |
| Eligibility | The platform defines eligible assets, inventory limits, pricing controls and settlement rules. |
| Quote | Investor submits a holding and quantity; LP supplies a time-limited quote where inventory and mandate permit. |
| Acceptance | Investor reviews price, gross 1.5% route charge and settlement terms, then accepts electronically. |
| Settlement | Payment, unit transfer, holder-register update and ledger reconciliation complete. |
| Economics | Gross route charge is 1.5%: 1.0% platform share and 0.5% LP compensation. |
| Reoffer | LP may reoffer eligible inventory. The 1% buyer fee on that LP resale is retained by the LP; the platform records no buyer-fee revenue on that specific resale. |
| Control | Exposure, inventory, pricing, transactions, conflicts and settlement are monitored and reported. |

# 30\. Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Capimax PropShare?

A digital non-blockchain platform for structured fractional participation in approved off-plan and under-construction real-estate projects and portfolios.

### Are PropShare units crypto tokens?

No. They are digitally administered contractual participation interests recorded in the platform ledger and supported by the applicable legal structure, agreements and holder register.

### Who can use the platform?

Eligible individual, family, developer, property-owner, broker, liquidity-provider, institutional and service-partner accounts.

### Who performs identity verification?

Sumsub provides the identity, business-verification and screening technology used by the onboarding workflow.

### How do I invest?

Create and verify an account, review an eligible property dossier, choose units and payment plan, accept documents, pay, and receive the allocation and certificate after settlement controls.

### Can I pay in cryptocurrency?

Yes, approved digital-asset payments can be processed through NOWPayments. The resulting PropShare holding remains a non-blockchain participation.

### Can I use cards or PayPal?

Supported online payments use Stripe and PayPal; verified bank-transfer routes are also available.

### What is the investor entry fee?

3% of the applicable participation basis.

### What is the annual management fee?

1% under the current fee card.

### What is the seller fee on the internal secondary market?

0.5% under the current fee card.

### What is the buyer fee on the internal secondary market?

1% under the current fee card.

### How does the LP exit charge work?

The gross route charge is 1.5%, comprising a 1.0% platform share and 0.5% LP compensation.

### Who performs financial analysis and valuation?

CIM Global Financial provides feasibility, valuation, financial due diligence, risk assessment and financial reporting services.

### Who performs legal due diligence?

LexCrest Global Legal handles the legal diligence, property-rights, SPV, contract, governance and transaction-documentation workstream.

### Who provides insurance services?

CoverTech Insurance and Assurax Insurance provide complementary asset, project, platform, cyber, operational and applicable client-money protection capabilities under issued policies.

### What is CPV?

Capimax Property Verification is the property evidence and verification layer available through Capimax Pro.

### How is ownership recorded?

Through the relevant legal structure and holder register, supported operationally by the PropShare central unit ledger and digital certificate.

### Is return guaranteed?

No. Projections are estimates, and investment performance depends on the property, project, market, counterparties and contractual structure.

### Is exit guaranteed?

No. PropShare provides controlled exit mechanisms, but execution depends on eligibility, rights, available demand and route conditions.

### What documents can investors access?

The approved property dossier includes relevant legal, financial, technical, valuation, insurance, risk, contract and progress documents according to role and access level.

### What does a broker do?

An approved broker introduces prospects through tracked referrals and approved materials; the broker does not replace investor verification or decision-making.

### What does a liquidity provider do?

An approved LP may quote for eligible holdings, acquire and settle inventory, and reoffer eligible units under controlled rules.

### How are installments managed?

The dashboard displays the agreed schedule, paid capital, due amounts, payment status and related documents.

### How do I verify my certificate?

Use its unique reference and the linked Capimax verification record to confirm the holder, property, units and current authoritative record.
